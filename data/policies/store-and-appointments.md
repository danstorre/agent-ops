# Store hours and appointments

## Hours
The store is open Monday to Friday from 10 am to 8 pm and Saturday from 10 am to 6 pm. It is closed on Sundays and public holidays.

## Appointments
Gait analysis (30 minutes, USD 40) and shoe fitting (20 minutes, free) can be booked Monday to Saturday during store hours. The last slot starts 30 minutes before closing. Cancellations are free up to 24 hours before the appointment.
