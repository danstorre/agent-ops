# Returns and exchanges

## Window
Unworn items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Running shoes that were worn outdoors can be exchanged once within 30 days under our "Run Test" guarantee, but not refunded.

## How to return
Start a return from the link in the order email or ask the assistant to hand you to a person. Return shipping is free for exchanges and costs USD 6 for refunds.

## Refund timing
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days after we receive the item.
