# Shipping

Northside Running Co. is a fictional store used for this demo.

## Delivery times
Orders placed before 2 pm ship the same business day. Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days. Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days.

## Costs
Standard shipping is free on orders of USD 80 or more; below that it costs USD 6. Express shipping costs USD 15 on any order.

## Tracking
Every order gets a tracking link by email and WhatsApp as soon as it ships.
